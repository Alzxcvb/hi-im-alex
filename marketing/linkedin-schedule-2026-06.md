## Content-type coverage + the social proof gap (added 2026-06-06)

From an `/isthisanything` run on a thread claiming four content types (pain naming, social proof, mechanism reveal, offer clarity) drive discovery calls if posted on weekly rotation. Evidence checked against real sources.

**What the research actually supports (and what it doesn't):**
- **Mechanism reveal / thought leadership** is the best evidenced type. Edelman + LinkedIn B2B Thought Leadership Study 2024 (~3,500 decision makers): 75% said a piece of thought leadership led them to research something new, ~90% more receptive to outreach from consistent publishers. 2025 follow up: 79% more likely to champion a vendor in an RFP. Vendor funded, self reported intent. Our mechanism posts (9, 11, 12, 13) are the strongest play and the data agrees. Ship the screen recordings.
- **Social proof** is a real mechanism (Cialdini 2008 field experiment ~26% lift, Nielsen 2015 66% trust online opinions, Spiegel/Northwestern 2017 ~270% but that's ecommerce reviews, not B2B services). Effect is real, the big numbers don't transfer to a LinkedIn testimonial.
- **Offer clarity** is supported (Gartner: buyers spend only ~17% of buying time with suppliers, 61 to 67% prefer rep free buying; TrustRadius: opaque pricing is the #1 buyer complaint, ~49%). Our explicit pricing (post 16) is on solid ground.
- **95/5 rule** (Ehrenberg Bass / LinkedIn B2B Institute): only ~5% of buyers are in market now, so most content should build memory, not sell. Our value heavy plan with sales concentrated in Week 4 already aligns. Note: the 4/1/1 and 80/20 ratios are folklore, cite 95/5 instead.
- **The packaged claim** ("post all four every week drives the most discovery calls") has no study behind it. The ingredients are evidenced, the recipe and the discovery call promise are not.

**Do NOT adopt the weekly rotation.** Our plan sequences the four types across Schwartz awareness stages (pain Weeks 1 to 2, mechanism Week 3, offer Week 4). That beats a flat "all four every week" and is backed by funnel logic plus 95/5. Posting offers weekly conflicts with our funnel stage discipline (we already got burned sneaking offers into Stage 1 posts). Keep the sequencing.

**The one real change: build the social proof supply.** This schedule bans fabricated stories, so we run only aggregate proof (300+ taught, 50+ setups, post 14) and zero outcome testimonials. Social proof is evidence backed and structurally missing. The fix is to create the supply, not to post proof we don't have:

1. After every paid Quick Start, capture one verifiable outcome plus name plus permission to share. One sentence is enough. Log it in `hi-im-alex/marketing/social-proof.md`.
2. Once 2 to 3 real ones exist, add one social proof post to the rotation at Stage 3 placement (proof belongs where the reader is comparing).
3. Mirror the same proof onto the hiimalex.ai site as a results section. That is the "elsewhere" this helps most, the site currently has no testimonials.
4. Until real client proof exists, the legitimate proof we can produce today is the demonstration recordings (posts 9, 11, 13). "Show Claude doing the real thing" is social proof without a client. Prioritize those.
