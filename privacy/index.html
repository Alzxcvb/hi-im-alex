Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

This site is run by Hi I'm Alex LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. This page says exactly what gets collected, why, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. If anything here is unclear, email me and ask.

The short version

I collect as little as I can get away with. I do not sell your data. I do not buy data about you. There is no advertising network on this site. The two things that leave my hands are your email address, which goes to Google Forms so I can send you what you asked for, and the text you type into the Automation Score tool, which goes to Anthropic so a model can score it.

What gets collected

When you just read the site

Two analytics tools run on every page:

ToolWhat it doesRun by
Vercel Web AnalyticsCounts page views and referrers. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you across other websites.Vercel Inc.
Microsoft ClarityRecords how people use the page, including mouse movement, scrolling, clicks and heatmaps. This can include a replay of your session on this site.Microsoft Corporation

Clarity is the more invasive of the two, so it deserves a plain statement: it can replay what you did on this site. It does not follow you to other sites. Microsoft masks text input by default. If you would rather not be recorded, a browser that blocks trackers, or a content blocker, will stop it, and the site still works normally.

When you ask for the starter guide

You give me a name and an email address. Those go to a Google Form that I own, so the list lives in my Google account. Your name, email, IP address and browser user agent also appear in this site's server logs, held by Vercel. I use the email to send you the guide and, sometimes, to follow up about the service. Reply with the word "stop" and I will remove you.

When you use the Automation Score tool

You type a description of your job. That text is sent to Anthropic's API so a Claude model can score it. Anthropic processes it to return the result. I do not keep the text you typed. What I do keep in the server logs is the resulting score, the role label, how many characters you typed, the token counts, and your IP address. Your share link carries your result inside the link itself, so nothing about it is stored on my side.

When you book a call

Booking runs on Calendly, and payment, when it applies, runs on Stripe. Each of those collects what it needs to do its job, under its own privacy policy. I never see or store your full card number.

My LinkedIn application

I run a private application registered with LinkedIn that does two things, both of them limited to my own account:

  • It publishes posts to my own LinkedIn feed from a queue I approve by hand.
  • Once LinkedIn approves the access, it will read the performance figures on my own posts, such as impressions and reactions, so I can tell which posts were worth writing. That part is not live yet.

This application acts only on content I authored. It does not read, collect, store or process data about any other LinkedIn member, it does not read anyone's profile, connections or messages, and it does not scrape LinkedIn. The only person whose LinkedIn data it touches is me. Nothing it reads is shared with a third party or used for advertising.

How long it is kept

Server logs age out on Vercel's own schedule, which is a matter of weeks. Email addresses on the mailing list stay until you ask me to remove yours. Analytics data is kept for as long as the analytics tool keeps it, which is 30 days for Clarity by default.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. If you are in the UK, the EU or California, you have that right in law. I will do it either way, and I will not charge you or ask why. Email me and it gets handled.

California residents: I have not sold or shared personal information for cross context behavioural advertising, and I do not.

Children

This site is for working adults. It is not aimed at anyone under 16, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it.
