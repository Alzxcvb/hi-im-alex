Refunds and Cancellations

Last updated 6 October 2026

This page covers the three services sold on hiimalex.ai by Hi I'm Alex LLC: the Quick Start, the Business Workflow Audit, and Audit + Build. It is written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, email hiimalexllc@gmail.com and I will answer in the same plain English.

The short version

Every service is paid before it starts. Cancel or move a booked session at least 24 hours before it begins and you get a full refund or a new time, your choice. With less than 24 hours notice, or if you do not turn up, the payment is kept. If I am the one who cancels, you get a full refund or a new time, your choice, every time.

Paying

The Quick Start is paid when you book it, by PayPal or card on the booking page. The Audit and Audit + Build are agreed by email and paid by PayPal invoice before the work starts. Prices are in US dollars.

Cancelling or moving a session

  • 24 hours or more before the start: email me and pick either a full refund or a new time. No questions, no fee.
  • Less than 24 hours before the start, or a no show: the payment is kept. I plan my day around the call and cannot fill the slot at short notice.
  • If I cancel or have to move it: full refund or a new time, whichever you prefer.

The Quick Start promise

The Quick Start comes with one promise: it runs by the end of the call, or I keep going at no extra charge. That promise is how a Quick Start that is not finished gets put right. It is not a refund, because the time has been spent on your setup.

The Audit and Audit + Build

The discovery session follows the same 24 hour rule as any booked session. If you are unhappy with the report, email me and say what is missing. I would rather fix it than argue about it.

How a refund reaches you

Refunds go back the way you paid, through PayPal. How quickly it shows up depends on PayPal and your bank.
