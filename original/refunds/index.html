Refunds and Cancellations

Last updated 28 October 2026

This page covers the four offers on hiimalex.ai/original, sold by Hi I'm Alex LLC: the Quick Start, the Business Workflow Audit, the Audit and AI Workflow Automation Build, and the Workshop in Your Business. It is written in plain English on purpose. If anything is unclear, email hiimalexllc@gmail.com and I will answer in the same plain English.

The short version

Everything is paid in full before it starts. Tell me at least 24 hours before your call, or 7 days before a workshop, and you can cancel for a full refund or move to a new time for free. If you do not turn up and have not cancelled, the payment is kept. Once a report or a build is delivered, there is no refund, but if it does not do what we agreed, I fix it at no charge. If I am the one who cancels, you get a full refund or a new time, your choice, every time.

Prices

  • Quick Start, $300. A 60 minute video call. Paid when you book.
  • Business Workflow Audit, $1,000.
  • Audit and AI Workflow Automation Build, $2,000.
  • Workshop in Your Business, $100 a seat. 15 seat minimum, so the smallest workshop is $1,500. Up to 30 seats.

Prices are in US dollars. The Quick Start is paid when you book it, by PayPal or card on the booking page. The Audit, the Audit and Build, and the Workshop are agreed by email and paid in full by PayPal invoice before the work starts.

How a refund reaches you

Refunds go back the way you paid, through PayPal. How quickly it shows up depends on PayPal and your bank.
