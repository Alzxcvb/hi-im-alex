Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This page covers the Originals pages at hiimalex.ai/original, run by Hi I'm Alex LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. It says what gets collected, why, who else sees it, and how to have it removed. If anything here is unclear, email me and ask.

The short version

I collect as little as I can. I do not sell your data. I do not buy data about you. There is no advertising network on these pages. The companies that see your data are the ones in the table below: Vercel hosts the pages, Cal.com runs booking, a video call tool runs the call, PayPal takes payment, Google holds my email, and Anthropic runs Claude.

What gets collected

When you read these pages. The offers page runs Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views and which site sent you. It does not use cookies and does not build a profile of you across other websites. Your IP address appears briefly in the server records kept by Vercel, which hosts the site.

When you book a Quick Start. Booking runs on Cal.com, which collects your name, email and the time you picked, under its own privacy policy. Payment is taken by PayPal on that booking page, under PayPal's privacy policy.

When you email me about the Audit, the Audit and Build, or a workshop. My email runs on Gmail, so your messages are held by Google. I keep them so we can arrange the work. Payment is by PayPal invoice. These pages never see card details of any kind.

During a session or workshop. The video call runs on the call tool named in your booking or invite. I will see your screen and whatever you choose to show me. I use it only to do the work you paid for, and I do not share it. Claude Code runs on your own Claude account, so what it reads is sent to Anthropic under your account and Anthropic's terms.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me for a copy of what I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. If you are in the UK, the EU or California, you have that right in law. I will do it either way, and I will not charge you or ask why. Email me and it gets handled.

California residents: I have not sold or shared personal information for ads based on your activity on other sites, and I do not.

Children

These pages are for working adults. They are not aimed at anyone under 16, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.
