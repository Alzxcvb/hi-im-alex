RFP drafting terms and refunds

Last updated 31 October 2026

These are the terms for the services on hiimalex.ai/rfp. They are short and in plain English on purpose. By telling me in writing to start on a draft, by sending me a solicitation to draft, or by paying an invoice, you agree to them. They apply only to these services. Where a signed agreement between us differs, the signed agreement wins.

Who you are dealing with

Hi I'm Alex LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, run by Alex Coffman. Email hiimalexllc@gmail.com.

What you are getting

  • A draft. A written draft of every section I can write for a solicitation you pick, in the order it asks for, with a compliance checklist that marks each requirement as mine or yours, and a one page fit note. One round of revisions. I aim to deliver in two business days once I have the solicitation and your files. The solicitation needs to be due at least five business days out when I get them. If two firms ask me to draft the same solicitation, I take the first written yes and tell the other I am not available for that one.
  • RFP response template, $500. A Word template built from your last three submittals, delivered in five business days after I have them.
  • AI setup for proposals, $1,000. Claude set up on your firm's own account and loaded with your proposal files, and a two hour video session with your team. Any Claude subscription or usage charge is between you and Anthropic and is not in my price.

What drafts cost

  • First draft, free. One free draft per firm, whether or not you submit it. I ask for a 20 minute debrief, your team's hours against your usual number, and permission to quote the result without naming your firm. Those three are requests, not a condition of keeping the draft.
  • Later drafts, flat fee. $1,800 for a standard SOQ or letter of interest up to about 20 pages. $3,500 for a full SF 330 or a large RFP. We agree which applies by email before I start.
  • You pay only for a draft you submit. The fee is due if any narrative section in the package your firm submits is mostly my text. You tell me by email when you submit, and the invoice is due in 14 days. If you set the draft aside and write your own, you owe nothing.

Prices are in US dollars. Fees are paid by PayPal invoice. The template and the setup are paid before the work starts.

No fee depends on winning

No fee here depends on an award, a shortlist, an interview or the value of any contract, and I will not agree to one. I do not contact any agency about your submission, and I do not act as your employee, agent or broker.

If a draft misses a requirement

If an agency's written rejection cites a submission requirement that the checklist marked as mine (format, section order, page limits, required narrative content), I redo your next draft for free. Items the checklist marks as yours (signatures, notarizations, forms you must execute, fees, delivery) are not covered. I never guarantee a win, a shortlist or an interview.

Your firm stays responsible

AI makes mistakes and so do people. A draft is a draft. I do not seal, stamp or sign anything and a draft is not engineering design or legal advice. You check every claim, name, number and project before you submit, you sign it, and you submit it. You are responsible for the solicitation's own rules, including any rule on outside help, and for showing me only files you are allowed to show me.

Your files

I use your files only for your work. I do not share them with another firm. They are processed through Claude, a product of Anthropic, under Anthropic's terms. I delete my copies when you ask. Anything I write for you is yours. I keep the general methods I bring to every client, which do not include your business information.

Refunds

  • Template and setup. Full refund if you cancel at least 24 hours before the setup session, or before I deliver the template. After delivery there is no refund, but I will fix anything that does not match what we agreed.
  • Paid drafts. You pay after you choose to submit, so there is nothing to refund unless you paid in error, in which case tell me and I will return it.

If something goes wrong

Tell me and I will put it right, starting with the promises above. Beyond that, my total responsibility to you for any service is limited to the amount you paid me for it. I am not responsible for lost contracts, lost profits or other indirect losses.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version is posted here with a new date at the top. The terms that apply to you are the ones that were live on the day you sent the solicitation or paid.
