Questions before you book

I'm not technical at all. Will I be lost?

No. Plenty of the 50+ people I have set up had never opened a terminal, the black window programmers type into. I talk you through each click, and you can stop me and ask at any point. No jargon. Stephen Roberts wrote on LinkedIn that I have “a talent for making it accessible to people who are not technical.”

What if it is not working by the end of the call?

Then I keep going at no extra charge. You are paying for it to actually work, not for advice. It is a promise to finish, not a refund, because the time goes into your setup. The details are on the refunds page.

What do I need before we start?

  • A Mac or Windows computer you are allowed to install software on.
  • An internet connection.
  • A paid Claude plan. Pro is $20 a month from Anthropic, and the free plan does not include Claude Code, which is what we install: the version of Claude that runs on your computer and can work with your files.
  • One task from your week that annoys you. We pick it together in the first few minutes.
  • What that task needs: for email replies, your Gmail or Outlook account and some past replies; for call notes, a recording or transcript saved on your computer; for a document you keep rewriting, 5 to 10 past examples.

Is it safe to share my screen with you?

You choose what to share and you can stop sharing at any time. When Claude works on a task, what it reads is sent to Anthropic to process, under Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your plan.

Can it mess something up?

AI can make mistakes, so you check its work before anything goes out. On the call I show you how to keep it from changing anything you cannot undo.

Will it work for my kind of business?

If you do the same kind of task every week on a computer, that is the kind of task this is built for. Good first tasks are drafting replies to the same emails, turning call notes into follow ups, and writing the same kind of document again and again, like proposals or reports. We pick one that can be running within the hour. Not sure yours fits? Email me and ask before you book.

What does it cost after the call? Am I signing up for something?

Nothing more to me. You pay once for the Quick Start, and it is complete on its own, with no subscription. The only ongoing cost is your own Claude plan, which you pay to Anthropic.

Are you part of Anthropic?

No. I am an independent consultant. Claude is Anthropic's product. I set it up with you and show you how to use it.

Is this a sales call in disguise?

No. The hour goes into setting up your AI. If I think one of the two bigger options at the bottom of this page would help, I may say so afterwards by email, and you are free to ignore it.

Can I move or cancel my session?

Yes. Email me at least 24 hours ahead to cancel or move it and you get a full refund or a new time, your choice. With less notice, or if you do not turn up, the payment is kept. If I cancel, you get a full refund or a new time, your choice. The full terms are on the refunds page.
